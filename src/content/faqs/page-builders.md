question: Do you use page builders like Elementor, Divi or WPBakery?
category: WordPress
order: 50
plain: No. I build custom themes with ACF or native Gutenberg blocks. Page builders add significant weight, lock your content into proprietary shortcodes and make future migration expensive. Custom themes are typically three to five times faster.
---

No — and this is a deliberate position rather than a preference.

Page builders add substantial weight to every page, lock your content inside proprietary
shortcodes, and make any future migration expensive or lossy. They also tend to encourage
inconsistency, because every page can be laid out differently.

Instead I build a custom theme with ACF field groups or native Gutenberg blocks that match your
actual content. Editors get clearly labelled fields rather than a blank canvas, which is
genuinely faster to work with and structurally cannot break a layout.

The performance difference is not marginal. Custom themes typically run three to five times
faster than the page-builder equivalent.
