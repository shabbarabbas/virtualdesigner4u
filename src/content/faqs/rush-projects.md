Yes — provided the deadline is realistic.

Tell me the date at the first conversation and I will give you an honest answer about whether
it is achievable, and if not, what would need to change to make it so. Usually that means
reducing scope for launch and adding the rest afterwards, which is almost always a better
outcome than rushing everything.

Genuine rush work — evenings and weekends, displacing other commitments — carries a surcharge
of 25–50% depending on how much reshuffling is involved. I will always tell you upfront rather
than quietly agreeing and then missing the date.
