Every project includes **30 days of post-launch support** at no additional cost. That covers
bugs, small adjustments and any questions your team has while getting used to the site.

After that you have two options. A monthly maintenance plan (from $150) covers updates,
backups, security monitoring and a block of development time for small changes. Or you can
simply get in touch when something comes up and I will quote it.

There is no obligation either way. Plenty of clients launch, run independently for a year, and
come back when they want something new.
