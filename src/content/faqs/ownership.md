You do — completely — once the final invoice is settled. That includes source code, design
files, and every asset created for the project.

There is no proprietary framework, no licence key, and nothing that stops another developer
picking the project up. If you decide to move on, I will hand over the repository, the
documentation and anything else needed without obstruction or an exit fee.

I take the view that clients should stay because the work is good, not because leaving is
expensive.
