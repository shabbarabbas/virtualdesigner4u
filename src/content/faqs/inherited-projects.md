Yes — inherited projects are a regular part of my work, including ones other developers have
declined.

I start with a **paid audit** of a few hours. I read the code, identify what is sound and what
is not, and give you a written, honest recommendation: repair, partially rebuild, or start over.

That recommendation is not always in my commercial interest. I have told clients their existing
site was fine and needed a few hundred dollars of fixes rather than the rebuild they came asking
for. You get the audit findings regardless of whether you continue with me.
