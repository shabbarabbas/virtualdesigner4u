Typical durations:

- **Landing page** — 1–2 weeks
- **Portfolio site** — 2–4 weeks
- **Custom WordPress build** — 3–6 weeks
- **Business website** — 4–8 weeks
- **HTML5 or Phaser game** — 5–12 weeks

You get a specific timeline in your written scope, not a range.

The single most common cause of delay is **content**. Text, photography and logo files arriving
two weeks later than planned will move the launch date by two weeks. I flag exactly what I need
and when at the start of the project so this is visible rather than a surprise.
