---
question: Should I use WordPress or a static site like Astro?
category: WordPress
order: 51
plain: WordPress suits sites with frequent content updates by non-technical teams, e-commerce, or complex membership needs. Static sites like Astro are faster, cheaper to host and far more secure, and suit marketing sites, portfolios and documentation. I recommend based on your situation, not preference.
---

It depends on how the site will actually be used, and I will recommend based on your situation
rather than what I enjoy building.

**WordPress makes sense when** several non-technical people publish regularly, you need
e-commerce, memberships or bookings, or your team already knows the admin interface.

**A static site like Astro makes sense when** content changes occasionally, speed matters a
great deal, or you want minimal maintenance. Static sites have no database to attack, no
plugins to patch, and hosting frequently costs nothing at all.

For a typical marketing site or portfolio, static is usually the better answer. For a busy
content operation with five editors, WordPress usually is. Both can be excellent — the mistake
is picking one by default.
