Indicative starting points:

- **Landing page** — from $700
- **Portfolio site** — from $1,000
- **Custom WordPress build** — from $1,500
- **Business website** — from $2,500
- **HTML5 or Phaser game** — from $2,000

These are genuine starting points rather than teaser figures. What moves the number is page
count, how much custom functionality is involved, and whether content and photography already
exist.

Every project is quoted at a **fixed price** after a scoping conversation. You know the total
before committing, and it does not change unless you ask for something we did not agree.
