Two structured revision rounds at the design stage and one after the build — all included in the
quoted price.

In practice most projects use fewer, because designs are reviewed as working pages in a browser
rather than as flat images. You catch far more when you can click through something on your own
phone.

The distinction I make is between **revisions** and **scope changes**. Refining what we agreed
is a revision. Adding a booking system that was never discussed is a scope change — I will tell
you clearly, quote it separately, and let you decide before any work happens.
