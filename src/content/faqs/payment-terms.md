For most projects: **40% to begin, 30% on design approval, 30% before launch**.

Projects running longer than about two months can be split into monthly milestone payments
instead, which is usually easier for budgeting on both sides.

I accept bank transfer, Wise and Payoneer, and can invoice in USD, GBP or EUR. Retainer and
maintenance clients are billed monthly in advance.

For smaller fixed-scope work under roughly $800, I usually just invoice 50% upfront and 50% on
completion — the milestone structure adds more admin than it is worth at that size.
