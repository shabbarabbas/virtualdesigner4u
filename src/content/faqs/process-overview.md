Six stages: discovery and scope, design direction, build, test and optimise, launch and
handover, then ongoing support.

The important part is that you get a **written scope with a fixed price and timeline before any
work begins**. No hourly surprises, no "that will be extra" conversations halfway through.

From the first week of development you have a staging link, so you can follow progress
continuously rather than waiting for a big reveal at the end. Most clients find this is the
single biggest difference from previous agency experiences.
