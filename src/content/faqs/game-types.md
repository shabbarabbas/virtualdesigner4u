Anything 2D: puzzle, arcade, platformer, endless runner, quiz, card, simulation and educational
games. Built with Phaser 3 and the HTML5 Canvas, they run in any modern browser with no
download, no plugin and no app store approval.

That makes them well suited to marketing campaigns, learning resources, product demos and games
embedded in an existing site or LMS.

**What I do not take on:** 3D games, large-scale multiplayer, or native console and mobile
titles. Those need different specialists, and I would rather refer you on than take work I
cannot do properly.
