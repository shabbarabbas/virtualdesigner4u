Directly with me. I design the site and write the code myself — there is no account manager
relaying your feedback to someone you never meet.

For larger projects I occasionally bring in a specialist for photography, illustration or
long-form copywriting. That only happens with your knowledge and agreement, and I remain your
single point of contact and the person accountable for the result.

The practical benefit is that the person who understands your project is always the person you
are talking to.
