## Overview

Ledger & Co advise high-net-worth individuals on long-term wealth management. Their clients
research carefully and are, appropriately, sceptical. The website needed to survive that scrutiny.

## Approach

The design process began by collecting every competitor site in the category and identifying
what they all had in common — then systematically avoiding it. What emerged was a direction
closer to a well-set financial publication than a typical services website.

Accessibility and compliance ran in parallel with design rather than following it, which is the
only way either gets done properly.

## Outcome

The site cleared compliance review on the first pass, and the firm reports that prospective
clients now arrive at meetings already familiar with the specific partner they want to work with.
