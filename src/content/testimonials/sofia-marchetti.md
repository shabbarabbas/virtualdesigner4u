We have used Shabbar as our white-label frontend partner for four years across more than twenty
client projects. He takes a Figma file and returns a build that matches it precisely, including
all the states and breakpoints designers forget to specify.

He also flags the things we got wrong — inconsistent spacing, contrast failures, a hover state
that would not work on touch. That feedback has genuinely improved how our design team works.
He is the only external developer we do not need to review line by line.
