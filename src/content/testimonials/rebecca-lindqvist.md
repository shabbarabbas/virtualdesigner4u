Our first attempt at this game failed because the developer built it on a high-end machine and
never tested on what schools actually own. Sixty percent of our target devices could not run it.

Shabbar asked for the exact device list before writing any code and treated the slowest one as
the requirement. The finished game runs at a locked 60fps on 2014 hardware and downloads in
under six seconds on throttled school WiFi. It is now in 180 schools and we have extended it
twice without needing to rewrite anything.
