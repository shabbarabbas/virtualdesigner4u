We inherited a half-finished platform when our previous development team walked away. Two other
developers looked at it and quoted us for a complete rebuild.

Shabbar spent two days reading the code, sent us an honest assessment of what was salvageable
and what was not, and had the critical bugs fixed within a week. He was clear that parts of it
would eventually need replacing and explained exactly why, rather than either overselling a
rewrite or pretending everything was fine. That honesty is why we still work with him.
