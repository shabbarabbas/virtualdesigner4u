Our site finally looks like our work. More importantly I can update it myself on a Friday
afternoon without worrying I will break something, which was never true before.

We had been quoted twice as much by a London agency for something that would have been another
page builder with a nicer skin. Shabbar built us a proper custom theme, removed eleven plugins,
and our homepage is now a third of the weight it was. Six months on we have published thirty
projects and never once needed to ask him for help.
