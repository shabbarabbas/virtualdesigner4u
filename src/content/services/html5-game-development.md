Browser games remove almost every barrier between you and a player. There is nothing to
download, nothing to approve, and no platform taking a cut — someone clicks a link and is
playing within seconds.

The engineering challenge is that "any browser, any device" includes a five-year-old tablet on
school WiFi. I build to that constraint from the start: tight asset budgets, sprite atlases,
pooled objects and a frame budget that holds at 60fps on modest hardware.

Controls are designed for both touch and keyboard from day one rather than retrofitted, which
is usually where ported games fall apart on mobile.
