The most expensive redesign mistake is not aesthetic — it is launching a beautiful new site
that silently drops half your organic traffic because URLs changed and nobody mapped the
redirects.

So I treat a redesign as a migration project that happens to include new design work. Before
anything else, I inventory every existing URL, identify which pages actually earn traffic and
links, and build a redirect map that preserves them.

Your analytics also inform the redesign itself. Existing data usually shows exactly where
visitors drop off — which is a far better brief than "make it look modern".
