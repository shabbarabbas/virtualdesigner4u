Websites decay. Plugins go unmaintained, certificates expire, PHP versions get deprecated, and
the contact form quietly stops delivering email six weeks before anyone notices.

Maintenance is the unglamorous work that prevents all of that. Updates are applied on staging
first, backups are tested rather than assumed, and monitoring means I usually know something
is wrong before you do.

Each plan includes development time for the small requests that would otherwise pile up — a
new team member, an updated price list, a seasonal banner. Most clients find this is the part
they value most.
