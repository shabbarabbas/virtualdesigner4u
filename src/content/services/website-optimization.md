Performance work goes wrong when people optimise by folklore. Enabling every toggle in a
caching plugin is not a strategy, and it frequently makes things worse.

I start by measuring — real profiling on real devices and connections — to find what is
genuinely costing you time. It is usually a short list: oversized images, render-blocking
third-party scripts, unbudgeted web fonts, and layout shift from elements without reserved
space.

You get a prioritised plan with the expected impact of each fix, then the implementation, then
a verified before-and-after benchmark. No vague promises, just numbers.
