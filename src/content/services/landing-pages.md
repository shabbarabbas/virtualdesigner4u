A landing page has one job. Every additional link, section or idea competing for attention
makes it measurably worse at that job.

I start from the traffic source and the promise that brought someone to the page, then build
the shortest credible path from that promise to the action you want. Proof points, objection
handling and the call to action are placed where scroll data says people actually look.

Because these pages usually sit behind paid traffic, speed is not cosmetic — it is budget.
A page that takes four seconds to load is quietly burning a meaningful share of your ad spend.
