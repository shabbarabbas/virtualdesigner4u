Some bugs are genuinely hard: they only appear on Safari, or only for logged-in users, or only
after a specific plugin update. These are the ones that get abandoned with a workaround taped
over them.

I work from reproduction outward. Before proposing any fix, I want to reliably trigger the
problem and understand the mechanism — because a fix you cannot explain is a fix that will
quietly stop working.

You get the repair plus a short written account of what went wrong and what would prevent a
recurrence. Frequently the underlying cause affects more than the one symptom you noticed.
