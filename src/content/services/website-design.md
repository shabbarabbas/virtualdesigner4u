Most websites fail not because they are ugly, but because they are unclear. Before I open a
design tool I want to know who is landing on the page, what they are trying to work out, and
what single action matters most to your business.

From there the design work is deliberate: a type scale that creates real hierarchy, spacing
that gives content room to breathe, and colour used with enough restraint that when something
is highlighted, it genuinely stands out.

You review designs as working pages in a browser rather than flat images, so you can click
through them on your own phone before committing to a build.
